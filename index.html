About Us.

Revolution Nepal Esports is a competitive esports organization built to unite Nepal’s most talented and driven gamers. We believe esports is more than playing games. It demands strategy, leadership, discipline, and the resilience to overcome every challenge.

MISSION
02 / MISSION

Our Mission.

To develop emerging talent, create meaningful competitive opportunities, and connect gamers across Nepal, while contributing to the growth of the nation’s esports industry.

VISION
03 / VISION

Our Vision.

To become a respected name in the esports scene, delivering unforgettable experiences and inspiring the next generation of Nepali gamers to pursue their passion without limits.
